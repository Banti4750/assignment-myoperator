# Findings — TalentFlow / Acme Corp

All numbers trace to a script in `queries/`. Re-run any of them against `data/talentflow.db`
(built by `queries/02_pull.py` + `queries/04_load_sqlite.py` from the live Airtable base).
Ordered by how much each should move the roadmap. Data-quality findings are included, not
split into a separate document — several of them directly change the number above them.

| # | Claim | Evidence (number + denominator) | Query ref | Confidence | What would change my mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The VP's 26.9% figure is exactly reproducible, but "biggest channel by a wide margin" is false — Job Board **ties** Referral for the most hires. | 7/26 hires from Job Board = 26.92%; 7/26 from Referral = 26.92%. | 3 | Job Board's specific weak point is interview→offer conversion, not screening or ghosting.
